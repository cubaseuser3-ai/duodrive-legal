Duo Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Deutsch

In short: Duo Drive has no account, no ads, no tracking and no servers of its own. What the app knows about you (destinations, favorites, parking spot, trips, settings) stays on your device. Some features send the minimum needed to the services listed below.

Provider

Stefan Fritschi, Switzerland
Email: info@my-tech.ch

Data that stays on your device

Recent and saved places, home and work, parking spot, trips, favorites, radio favorites and podcast subscriptions, home screen layout and all settings. They are never sent to us and are removed when you delete the app.
